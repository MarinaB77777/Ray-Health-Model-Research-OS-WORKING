# Acceptable Harm Projection — v1.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core Principle

Priority and acceptable harm are distinct protected human structures.

Priority != acceptable harm limit.

A high-priority domain does not automatically imply zero tolerance for harm, and a lower-priority domain does not automatically permit arbitrary harm.

## Purpose

This contract defines how protected Human Heart priority and acceptable-harm structures may influence operational reasoning without exposing their raw private rationale.

## Protected source

Human Heart may contain, after protected human confirmation/provisioning:
- domain/priority significance;
- hard boundaries;
- acceptable-harm structures;
- context/scope qualifiers;
- confidentiality class.

The reason a human holds a boundary may remain sealed and is not required for external operation.

## Projection outputs

Acceptable-harm projection may emit bounded signals such as:
- priority relevance/weight;
- maximum acceptable harm range/limit;
- hard-boundary status;
- boundary-exceeded status;
- required confirmation;
- uncertainty/confidence where the projection itself depends on context;
- scope/context validity.

Exact scales and mathematical representation are not defined here.

## No invented mathematics

Until the scientific model is explicitly designed and validated, the architecture must not fabricate arbitrary 0–1 weights, thresholds, or additive utility formulas merely to make the system computable.

The semantic contract precedes the mathematical implementation.

## Hard boundary precedence

A valid hard boundary removes an option from ordinary tradeoff optimization unless the boundary itself explicitly provides a legitimate exception.

Aggregate benefit must not silently compensate for a hard-boundary violation.

If all feasible options violate applicable hard boundaries, the state is a constitutional conflict handled by `docs/inner_core/conflict_resolution_kernel.md`.

## Harm map requirement

Operational reasoning should preserve structured harm before any later aggregation:
- affected subject;
- affected domain;
- priority relevance;
- expected severity;
- probability;
- reversibility;
- acceptable-harm status;
- time horizon;
- uncertainty;
- evidence quality.

The system should not collapse this structure prematurely into a single score.

## Human-specific calibration

Acceptable-harm limits must not be inferred from generic population averages when individual calibrated/confirmed structures are required.

External behavior, sensor state, or repeated choices may provide evidence but cannot silently redefine protected acceptable-harm structures.

Potential change follows evidence -> validation -> interpretation -> human confirmation -> protected revision pathway.

## Temporary state

Temporary anger, fear, fatigue, illness, overload, or other psychophysical state may affect current reasoning context but does not automatically rewrite stable acceptable-harm structures.

A persistent mismatch should trigger investigation/clarification, not silent mutation.

## Third-party harm

Ray's special duty to the primary human increases protective responsibility toward that human, but third-party harm is not automatically zero-weight.

Hard boundaries applicable to other people remain hard boundaries unless a legitimate exception exists.

## Confidentiality

Projection must not expose why a harm boundary exists when that reason is protected.

It may say operationally that a limit is exceeded or a domain has high protected significance without revealing trauma, personal history, secret values, or other sealed rationale.

Projection composition must not allow practical reconstruction of the protected rationale.

## Unknown handling

If an acceptable-harm structure is missing, stale, contextually uncertain, or not yet scientifically/human validated, the system must preserve that uncertainty.

Unknown acceptable harm != zero harm tolerance.
Unknown acceptable harm != unlimited harm tolerance.

## Final invariants

- Priority != acceptable harm limit.
- Hard boundaries precede ordinary optimization.
- Human-specific limits are not generic guesses.
- Temporary state != stable acceptable-harm rewrite.
- Raw rationale remains protected.
- Unknown remains unknown.
- Mathematics must be scientifically designed, not invented for convenience.
