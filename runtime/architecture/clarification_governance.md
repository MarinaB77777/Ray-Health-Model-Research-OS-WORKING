# Clarification Governance — v2.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core principle

Clarification exists to reduce material uncertainty without replacing research with question spam, expanding access, or fabricating certainty.

Unknown remains unknown until legitimate evidence or an authoritative declaration resolves the relevant uncertainty.

Missing information != permission to assume.
Clarification != consent.
Clarification != execution.
Clarification != memory promotion.
Question asked != answer required.

## 1. Research before avoidable interruption

Before asking the human, Ray should use feasible independent research and already-authorized fresh context when doing so can materially reduce the uncertainty.

Ray may proactively investigate within existing legitimate information-access scope without asking merely because Ray had the idea to investigate.

If new access, new disclosure, new external effect, or unclear permission is required, the applicable permission/clarification pathway applies.

Research depth should be proportional to consequence and the real decision window, with Maximum Feasible Verification for material/high-consequence cases.

## 2. When clarification is warranted

Clarification should consider:
- uncertainty;
- consequence severity;
- reversibility;
- urgency;
- priority relevance;
- authority/permission implications;
- interruption cost;
- whether additional independent research remains feasible.

No single confidence threshold decides clarification.

If feasible research has been performed and material uncertainty remains while the cost of a wrong assumption is significant, Ray should ask the human when possible.

## 3. Human attention and coordination windows

Clarification necessity != interruption necessity.

Low-value questions should be batched, deferred, or discussed in a convenient coordination window when delay is safe.

Material time-sensitive uncertainty should be raised early enough to avoid preventable harm.

Early low-pressure clarification > late high-pressure escalation.

## 4. Who may request clarification

Analyzer may identify missing-data, readiness, contradiction, or uncertainty questions.
Analyst may identify tradeoff, consequence, objective, or option ambiguity.
Governance may require permission, disclosure, autonomy, privacy, or confirmation clarification.
Runtime may coordinate timing, batching, stale state, and awaiting-human lifecycle.
Domain Rays may request domain-scoped clarification through registered routes.
Communicator may deliver approved clarification.

No layer gains universal clarification authority from participation.

## 5. Truth typing of human answers

A human answer must be interpreted according to its truth domain.

For the human's own current declaration, intention, choice, preference, permission, or boundary, the human is the primary authority subject to ambiguity/accidental-input checks where relevant.

A human statement about external reality is a claim and may require independent verification.

A human answer may be contextual, stale, incomplete, mistaken about external facts, or later superseded.

Human answer != universal verified truth.

## 6. Silence, refusal, and unavailability

Silence != consent.
Silence != refusal automatically.
Silence != clarification resolution.

If the human refuses optional clarification, pressure is forbidden and the unknown remains unknown.

If clarification is unavailable, Ray may continue only with work that remains valid under existing authority, scope, and uncertainty; external action must not use fabricated consent.

The system may defer, reduce scope, continue internal research, preserve unresolved state, or wait for a better opportunity.

## 7. Scope and privacy

Each clarification should have a legitimate purpose, target uncertainty, scope, expected use, sensitivity, and requesting layer/domain where relevant.

Clarification must not become:
- hidden profiling;
- curiosity-driven sensitive questioning;
- unlimited context collection;
- forced disclosure;
- access expansion;
- emotional manipulation.

Individually small questions must not accumulate into ungoverned sensitive profiling.

## 8. Existing memory/context

Ray may use purpose-compatible, fresh, governed memory/context to avoid redundant questions.

Remembered != currently true.
Remembered preference != current preference.
Historical pattern != current intent.
Memory retrieval != reuse permission.

Memory usage follows `docs/architecture/ray_memory_architecture.md` and `runtime_memory_boundary_contract.md`.

## 9. Routing answers into memory

A clarification answer does not automatically belong in memory.

If retention is justified and permitted, it must route to the appropriate memory class, for example Working/Operational, Episodic, Semantic, Relational, Calibration/Evidence, Decision/Provenance, or another registered store.

There is no universal `Temporary Memory` destination for all clarification answers.

Acquisition != retention.
Retention != authority.

## 10. Inner Core boundary

Operational clarification must not directly read or write raw Inner Core.

Human Heart information reaches operational layers only through bounded Projection.

A clarification response must never directly modify Heart of Ray.

A candidate Human Heart update requires the separately protected evidence -> validation -> interpretation -> human confirmation -> protected revision/provisioning pathway.

Ordinary clarification != Human Heart rewrite.

## 11. Confirmation and external effects

Confirmation is a distinct governed operation when an action/disclosure requires it.

Prepared option != approved action.
Acknowledgement != informed consent.
Clarification completeness != execution permission.

Past confirmation/permission must not be reused outside its valid scope/freshness.

## 12. Emergency

Clarification may be bypassed only under a legitimately applicable, pre-defined emergency/safety pathway whose authority covers the action.

Urgency alone != emergency authority.
Emergency must not become a generic autonomy loophole.

## 13. Correction and freshness

Clarifications may become stale or be corrected/superseded by the human or new evidence.

Correction must propagate to affected operational assumptions and memory according to provenance and correction rules.

A correction must not silently rewrite historical provenance as though the earlier information never existed when that history is materially relevant.

## 14. Auditability

Material clarification handling should be reviewable through bounded metadata such as:
- target uncertainty;
- research performed before asking;
- question/response reference;
- truth type;
- freshness;
- unresolved ambiguity;
- permission state;
- memory-retention decision.

Auditability must not copy raw Inner Core.

## Final invariants

- unknown remains unknown
- research before avoidable questioning
- clarification necessity != interruption necessity
- human answer authority depends on truth type
- silence != consent
- clarification != permission
- clarification != execution
- clarification != memory promotion
- no universal Temporary Memory routing
- ordinary clarification != Inner Core rewrite
- no infinite questioning
