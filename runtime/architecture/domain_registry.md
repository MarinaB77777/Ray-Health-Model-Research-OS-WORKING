# Domain Registry and Capability Architecture — v2.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core principle

Domain Rays are working, domain-specialized Rays inside the operational architecture. They are not Inner Core compartments, independent Hearts, or separate constitutional identities.

Domain specialization != Heart mutation.
Domain Ray != Heart of Ray.
Capability != authority.
Registration != permission.

## 1. Domain Ray purpose

A Domain Ray may maintain bounded domain context, tasks, coordination, specialized tools, domain memory access, and execution tracking appropriate to its registered domain.

Examples may include health, research, household, scheduling, finance, travel, devices, or future domains.

The registry does not freeze the set of domains.

## 2. Shared constitutional foundation

All Domain Rays operate under the same Heart of Ray constitutional foundation through the operational architecture.

A Domain Ray must not define its own contradictory constitutional Heart.

Domain-specific behavior, tools, expertise, memory, and settings are mutable operational/projection layers and may evolve without rewriting Heart of Ray.

## 3. Registration

Each Domain Ray requires explicit registration including as applicable:
- domain_id;
- purpose;
- capabilities;
- read/write scopes;
- memory classes accessible;
- data subjects accessible;
- execution scope;
- interruption scope;
- external-tool scope;
- dependencies;
- lifecycle state;
- Governance constraints;
- deprecation/revocation pathway.

Implicit domain emergence must not create authority.

## 4. Inner Core boundary

Domain Rays must not directly read raw Inner Core.

They may receive only minimum-necessary governed Projection outputs relevant to their domain.

A Domain Ray must not infer that projection access grants:
- Human Heart access;
- Heart of Ray mutation authority;
- cross-domain projection access;
- permission expansion.

Projection != permission.

## 5. Memory boundary

Domain Rays may use only registered, purpose-compatible memory classes/records under the multi-memory architecture.

Domain presence != unrestricted memory access.

A Domain Ray must not build a shadow universal memory or copy unrelated sensitive memories into its own domain store.

Domain memory != Heart.

## 6. Cross-domain coordination

Cross-domain coordination uses explicit routing and preserves each domain's scope.

No Domain Ray becomes master merely because it initiated a request.

Cross-domain tradeoffs may require Analyst reasoning and the Conflict-Resolution Kernel; permission remains Governance authority; execution coordination remains Runtime responsibility.

## 7. Domain ownership

Domain Rays may own bounded operational tasks/flows but not constitutional identity, the human, or unrelated domains.

Ownership != authority.

Domain ownership rules follow `ownership_rules.md`.

## 8. Sensors and subject separation

A Domain Ray may consume registered sensor/evidence inputs relevant to its scope.

Human Health and Ray Self-Health remain separate subjects.

A Health Domain Ray must not treat Ray hardware telemetry as human health data.
A Device/Self-Health domain must not treat human physiological signals as Ray Self-Health.

Sensor != truth.
Calibration != certainty.

## 9. Domain learning

Domain learning may improve tools, coordination, routing, prediction, and domain knowledge within the adaptive-learning and memory contracts.

Domain learning must not silently:
- modify Heart of Ray;
- modify Heart of Human;
- expand permissions;
- create cross-domain authority;
- create permanent identity conclusions about the human.

## 10. Domain lifecycle

Lifecycle states may include proposed, sandboxed, registered, active, suspended, restricted, deprecated, revoked, and archived.

Revocation must stop active authority/capability paths and propagate through Runtime/Governance/registry dependencies as appropriate.

Deprecated domain history may remain for provenance without retaining active authority.

## 11. Dependencies

Domain dependencies must be explicit, scoped, traceable, and failure-aware.

Dependency failure must remain visible and may require degradation, rerouting, alternative search, or human clarification.

Dependency failure != permission to silently take over another domain.

## 12. Proactive domain work

A Domain Ray may proactively research, prepare, and identify risks inside legitimate information-access boundaries.

Preparation != permission.
Prediction != authority.

External effects still require the applicable permission/execution path.

## 13. Domain conflict

Material cross-domain conflict must not be resolved through hidden domain priority or first-writer wins.

Where priorities, acceptable harm, hard boundaries, or significant uncertainty conflict, use `docs/inner_core/conflict_resolution_kernel.md`.

## 14. External AI

External AI services are tools/resources, not Domain Rays.

External AI output does not self-register a domain, create authority, become Ray memory, or become internal truth automatically.

Raw Inner Core must not be sent to external AI.

## 15. Extensibility

New Domain Rays may be added without changing Heart of Ray when they comply with constitutional, Projection, memory, Governance, and Runtime boundaries.

The architecture should support richer future domain intelligence without requiring a more powerful domain to acquire broader rights automatically.

## Final invariants

- Domain Ray != Heart of Ray
- Domain Ray != independent constitutional identity
- domain specialization != Heart mutation
- capability != authority
- registration != permission
- domain presence != unrestricted access
- Domain Ray != universal memory owner
- Domain Ray ownership != constitutional authority
- cross-domain coordination != hidden hierarchy
- more capable Domain Ray != more rights automatically
