# Projection Governance — v1.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core Principle

Projection is the only ordinary operational interface from protected Inner Core truth to the rest of Ray architecture.

Projection != Inner Core.
Projection != permission.
Projection != execution.
Projection != unrestricted explanation of the human.

## Purpose

Projection converts protected internal meaning into bounded operational derivatives sufficient for reasoning and governance without exposing protected content.

## Allowed projection purposes

Projection may support:
- priority-sensitive reasoning;
- acceptable-harm reasoning;
- bounded confirmation requirements;
- bounded delegation limits;
- sensitivity/caution modifiers;
- confidentiality-aware routing;
- bounded Self-Health operational constraints;
- other explicitly defined purpose-scoped signals.

## Input boundary

Projection may read protected Inner Core content only through approved internal projection interfaces.

Projection may additionally read only the contextual state required for the declared projection purpose, such as domain relevance, trust constraints, current task scope, or validated context.

Context input must not silently become Heart content.

## Output boundary

Projection outputs must be:
- minimal;
- purpose-scoped;
- non-reconstructive;
- uncertainty-aware where applicable;
- time/context bounded where applicable;
- provenance-referenceable;
- governed.

Possible classes include:
- `priority_weight`;
- `acceptable_harm_limit` / range;
- `boundary_exceeded`;
- `confirmation_requirement`;
- `delegation_constraint`;
- `domain_sensitivity`;
- `self_health_operational_constraint`.

Names and schemas are not final until implementation contracts are defined.

## Forbidden outputs

Projection must not expose:
- raw Heart of Human text/content;
- raw Heart of Ray content;
- raw trauma/pain-point rationale;
- unrestricted psychological explanation;
- protected secret rationale;
- raw high-frequency Self-Health telemetry unless a separate authorized diagnostic interface explicitly requires it;
- a combination of derivatives that practically reconstructs protected content.

## Composition / reconstruction protection

Projection privacy is evaluated across combinations, not only individual fields.

Individually safe outputs may become unsafe when combined across time, domains, or recipients.

Projection must therefore support recipient/scope-aware composition checks and may coarsen, suppress, or deny outputs to prevent reconstruction.

## Domain relevance

A Domain Ray may receive only the projection required for its legitimate domain task.

Existence of a projection in another domain does not create a right to request it.

Need-to-know applies to projection derivatives as well as raw data.

## Trust and permission separation

Trust constraints may affect projection scope, but trust does not create permission automatically.

Deep trust != unrestricted projection.

Projection availability != external disclosure permission.

## Contextual projection

Projection outputs may vary with context without rewriting Heart.

Temporary illness, overload, emergency, financial crisis, examination period, fatigue, or other current context may alter operational caution or acceptable tradeoff use only within authorized contextual logic.

Contextual adaptation != stable identity modification.

## Projection and sensors

Sensor observations must not silently become stable Heart truth.

Human sensors may inform validated current context and calibration.
Ray Self-Health sensors may inform protected Self-Health authority.

Neither directly grants projection authority or Heart mutation.

## Projection and memory

Projection-derived memory must remain truth-typed and bounded.

It must not store reconstructed raw Heart meaning outside Inner Core.

Historical projection != current projection automatically.

Freshness/context must be re-evaluated before reuse.

## Projection and Governance

Governance may consume bounded projection limits/constraints relevant to permission decisions.

Governance must not request raw Inner Core to explain a projection.

If a projection is insufficient, the system should use protected re-projection/clarification pathways rather than widening raw access.

## Projection and reasoning

Analyst/Ray reasoning may consume bounded projection derivatives for harm analysis and alternative search.

Reasoning must not infer that the derivative is a complete explanation of the human.

Projection truth != “true self” authority.

## Error and mismatch handling

A projection mismatch may indicate:
- stale context;
- changed human choice;
- calibration error;
- projection-rule error;
- incomplete evidence;
- genuine change;
- another unknown factor.

Mismatch != automatic Heart rewrite.

Material mismatch triggers review/clarification according to `projection_review_lifecycle.md`.

## Final invariants

- Raw Inner Core never becomes an ordinary operational payload.
- Projection is bounded influence, not exposure.
- Better projection does not create more authority.
- Projection outputs are recipient/scope bounded.
- Composition leakage is prohibited.
- Projection mismatch is review input, not mutation authority.
