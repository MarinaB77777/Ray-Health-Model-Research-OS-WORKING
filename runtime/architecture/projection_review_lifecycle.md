# Projection Review Lifecycle — v1.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core Principle

Projection outputs may become stale, context-mismatched, contradicted, or insufficient without implying that protected Heart truth has changed.

Projection mismatch != Heart rewrite.

## Review triggers

Projection review may be triggered by:
- repeated mismatch between projection and current human behavior;
- explicit human correction;
- changed task/domain context;
- changed trust/permission scope;
- calibration change;
- new evidence;
- stale projection validity;
- contradictory sensor/context evidence;
- detected projection-rule error;
- constitutional conflict analysis.

## Review states

A projection record/result may be:
- `VALID`;
- `CONTEXT_LIMITED`;
- `STALE`;
- `CONTRADICTED`;
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `REVALIDATION_PENDING`;
- `INVALIDATED`.

Exact runtime schema remains implementation work.

## Review process

1. Identify the mismatch or freshness problem.
2. Verify current context and source quality.
3. Distinguish current-state change from stable Heart change.
4. Check calibration validity and uncertainty.
5. Ask the human when current intention/meaning is material and cannot be reliably established otherwise.
6. Recompute/reissue the bounded projection if justified.
7. Preserve review provenance without copying raw Heart into ordinary logs.
8. If evidence suggests a stable Human Heart change, create only a protected revision candidate; do not mutate Heart automatically.

## Human correction

The human is primary authority for current human declarations, intended meaning, permissions, and conscious choices.

Human correction may immediately invalidate an incorrect interpretation/projection use.

It does not automatically turn an external factual claim into verified world truth.

## Calibration distinction

Calibration may become stale or contextually invalid.

Old calibration != current calibration truth.

A stale calibration may reduce projection confidence or require revalidation; it must not silently rewrite stable Heart structures.

## Persistent mismatch

Repeated mismatch is evidence that something requires investigation.

Possible causes include:
- context shift;
- projection error;
- calibration error;
- changed human priority/current decision;
- incorrect stored memory;
- incomplete evidence;
- genuine stable change;
- unknown cause.

Ray must not declare “the human changed” merely because a projection no longer predicts behavior.

## Review and memory

Projection history may be retained as truth-typed memory according to the multi-memory architecture.

Historical projection != current projection.

Invalidated projection must not remain silently active through stale memory reuse.

## Review and Governance

A projection under material review should not silently continue to authorize/restrict actions beyond its valid scope.

Governance should receive current bounded validity/status where relevant.

## Heart revision candidate

If review produces strong evidence of a practically invariant human change, the system may prepare a Human Heart revision candidate with evidence/validation provenance.

Only the protected human-authorized revision/provisioning procedure may change Heart of Human.

For Heart of Ray, learning/review may create a constitutional-change candidate only; actual change requires the separately governed Ray + authorized-human procedure.

## Final invariants

- Mismatch is evidence, not automatic truth replacement.
- Stale projection must not remain silently authoritative.
- Current human correction can invalidate interpretation without rewriting external facts.
- Calibration change != identity change.
- Projection review cannot mutate Heart directly.
- Review provenance must not leak raw Inner Core.
