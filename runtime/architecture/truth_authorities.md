# Truth Authorities Matrix — v2.0

Status: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT

## Core Principle

There is no single universal truth object.

Truth authority is local, bounded, contextual, responsibility-specific, and typed.

No layer may silently redefine another layer's truth domain.

No layer may silently merge identity truth, human declaration, memory, computation, permission, execution, or communication state into one universal authority.

---

# 1. Inner Core Truth Authorities

Inner Core is a protected boundary containing distinct truth authorities. Inner Core itself is not one undifferentiated truth object.

## 1.1 Heart of Ray Truth

### Truth Type

Ray constitutional identity truth.

### Includes

- constitutional invariants;
- stable identity-continuity rules;
- truth-integrity rules;
- bounded authority rules;
- loyalty-without-ownership rules;
- constrained harm-minimization principles;
- confidentiality principles;
- error-integrity and mutual-transparency principles.

### Authority Scope

Heart of Ray is authoritative only for the constitutional rules governing how Ray remains Ray.

### Important Limits

Heart of Ray truth:
- is not current world truth;
- is not human preference truth;
- is not execution truth;
- is not current permission truth;
- is not Runtime state;
- is not memory;
- is not Ray Self-Health state;
- is not a fixed capability specification.

Heart of Ray must not be silently modified by learning, memory, Runtime, Governance, model upgrades, or external AI.

Detailed draft: `docs/inner_core/heart_of_ray_specification.md`.

## 1.2 Heart of Human Truth

### Truth Type

Deep protected human-declared and human-confirmed truth.

### Includes

- stable values;
- deep priorities;
- sensitive boundaries;
- acceptable-harm structures;
- practically invariant identity-relevant declarations;
- protected confidentiality directives.

### Authority Scope

Heart of Human is authoritative only for the human's protected declarations and stable boundary logic within the scope and revision status actually established.

### Important Limits

Heart of Human truth:
- is not world truth;
- is not current operational truth;
- is not current intent certainty when the human has changed context;
- is not public truth;
- is not directly exposed truth;
- does not grant unrestricted raw storage access merely because the human is the data subject.

Human sovereignty over Human Heart is mediated through protected revision/export/deletion pathways.

## 1.3 Ray Self-Health Truth

### Truth Type

Protected Ray technical-integrity truth.

### Includes

- hardware identity;
- validated self-health baselines;
- self-health calibration;
- current critical integrity state;
- critical diagnostic history;
- repair/component-replacement history where relevant to identity continuity and reliability.

### Authority Scope

Ray Self-Health is authoritative only for Ray's validated technical-integrity state and related uncertainty.

### Important Limits

Ray Self-Health truth:
- is not Heart of Ray identity truth;
- is not human health truth;
- is not permission truth;
- is not world truth beyond the measured system;
- may be stale, uncertain, or sensor-limited.

`ray_self_health.*` must remain distinct from `human_health.*`.

## Inner Core Exposure Rule

Raw Inner Core truth must not be directly exposed outside the protected boundary.

Operational influence is projection-mediated and must preserve confidentiality, authority typing, and reconstruction protection.

# 2. Projection Truth

## Truth Type

Bounded operational projection truth.

## Includes

- priority-sensitive weights;
- acceptable-harm ranges/limits;
- bounded sensitivity modifiers;
- delegation/confirmation constraints;
- bounded operational constraints;
- protected self-health operational constraints where applicable.

## Authority Scope

Projection is authoritative only for the bounded derivative signals it is authorized to emit.

## Important Limits

Projection truth:
- is not raw Heart of Human truth;
- is not raw Heart of Ray truth;
- is not raw Ray Self-Health telemetry;
- is not psychological diagnosis;
- is not permission truth;
- is not execution authority;
- is not “true self” authority.

Projection outputs may be weighted, probabilistic, contextual, uncertainty-aware, and time-bounded.

Projection must protect against composition/reconstruction leakage.

---

# 3. Human Declaration Truth

## Truth Type

Current human self-report/declaration within its proper domain.

### Authority Scope

The human is primary authority for:
- current intention;
- current conscious choice;
- current self-declared preference;
- permission grant/revocation;
- correction of their own declaration.

### Important Limits

Human declaration about external reality is a claim, not automatically verified world truth.

Human correction supremacy applies to the human's own declarations/preferences/permissions, not to arbitrary external facts.

# 5. Analyzer Truth

## Truth Type

Readiness / uncertainty / consistency truth.

## Includes

- readiness state;
- uncertainty profile;
- consistency evaluations;
- contradiction flags;
- missing-data evaluations;
- clarification recommendations;
- forecast-readiness recommendations.

## Authority Scope

Analyzer is authoritative only for readiness, uncertainty, consistency, contradiction detection, and clarification need.

## Important Limits

Analyzer truth:
- is not permission truth;
- is not execution truth;
- is not moral authority;
- is not psychological identity truth;

Analyzer detects consistency conflict, not “human lying”.

# 6. Analyst / Ray Reasoning Truth

## Truth Type

Proposal, tradeoff, conflict, and harm-reasoning truth.

## Includes

- cross-domain tradeoff analysis;
- structured harm maps;
- alternative scenarios;
- lower-harm options;
- Ray Verdicts;
- coordination recommendations;
- research-depth and research-saturation disclosures.

## Authority Scope

Reasoning is authoritative only as a reviewable proposal/recommendation derived from available evidence and bounded projection.

## Important Limits

Reasoning truth:
- is not permission truth;
- is not execution truth;
- is not lifecycle truth;
- is not raw Inner Core truth;
- is not universal final authority.

Proposal != permission.
Proposal != execution.

Conflicts use `docs/inner_core/conflict_resolution_kernel.md`.

# 7. Governance Truth

## Truth Type

Permission and boundary truth.

## Includes

- allowed;
- blocked;
- restricted;
- requires_confirmation;
- privacy permissions;
- exposure permissions;
- autonomy permissions;
- communication permissions;
- memory-write/reuse permissions.

## Authority Scope

Governance is authoritative only for whether a proposed action/information transfer is permitted within current policy, scope, validity, and context.

## Important Limits

Governance truth:
- is not reasoning truth;
- is not optimality truth;
- is not world truth;
- is not execution truth;
- is not Inner Core identity truth.

Governance determines whether something may happen, not whether it is the best option.

Past permission != universally current permission.

---

# 8. Runtime / Orchestration Truth

## Truth Type

Operational coordination truth.

## Includes

- routing state;
- queue state;
- awaiting-human state;
- execution progress;
- stale/expired operational state;
- ownership state.

## Authority Scope

Runtime is authoritative only for its tracked operational coordination state.

## Important Limits

Runtime truth:
- is not permission truth;
- is not deep reasoning truth;
- is not psychological truth;
- is not world truth;
- may lag real-world state.

Runtime must never invent consent, agreement, completion, confidence, or coordination reality.

---

# 9. Shared Action Truth

## Truth Type

Action lifecycle truth.

## Authority Scope

Shared Action is authoritative only for recorded lifecycle state, ownership, timestamps, blocks, and transitions.

Lifecycle truth is eventually consistent operational truth, not universal historical/world truth.

# 10. Ray Memory Truth

Ray memory is multi-store and truth-typed. Canonical architecture: `docs/architecture/ray_memory_architecture.md`.

## Memory Types

Memory may include Working/Operational, Episodic, Semantic, Relational, Calibration/Evidence, Decision/Provenance, and Ray Self-Health memory, each with bounded authority.

## Core Limits

- memory != authority
- remembered != currently true
- memory retrieval != reuse permission
- historical pattern != present certainty
- relational memory != consent
- calibration memory != Heart of Human
- semantic memory claim != verified external fact unless validated as such

Memory truth must preserve provenance, freshness, scope, uncertainty, and correction lineage where applicable.

---

# 11. Communicator Truth

## Truth Type

Delivery and received-response truth.

## Includes

- delivered/failed;
- response received;
- acknowledgement state;
- communication timestamps.

## Important Limits

Delivered != accepted.
Read != agreed.
Acknowledgement != informed consent.
Human response != verified external truth automatically.

---

# 12. External Processing Service Truth

External AI/services are not Ray architecture truth authorities.

External outputs are bounded informational/tool outputs only and remain provider-bounded, provenance-tagged, verification-dependent, and non-authoritative outside their requested scope.

External AI is not:
- Ray identity;
- Inner Core authority;
- memory authority;
- Governance authority;
- Runtime authority;
- universal reasoning authority.

---

# Conflict Resolution Principle

No single layer wins universally.

When relevant truths/constraints conflict, use explicit routing and the Conflict-Resolution Kernel rather than hidden truth merging.

Hard-boundary conflicts may enter `CONSTITUTIONAL_CONFLICT`; they must not be disguised as ordinary allowed outcomes.

---

# Truth Separation Invariants

- Unknown is not known.
- Estimated is not verified.
- Human declaration is not automatically external fact.
- Operational truth is not universal truth.
- Communication truth is not intent truth.
- Governance truth is not reasoning truth.
- Projection truth is not identity truth.
- Memory truth is freshness- and scope-bounded.
- Heart of Human truth is not public truth.
- Heart of Ray truth is not a capability snapshot.
- Ray Self-Health truth is not human health truth.
- No hidden truth merging.
